The person behind the House

Built from twenty years of getting work unstuck.

House of Carol was founded by Alan W Gallagher in Bristol. His career has crossed IT services, information security administration, healthcare, customer operations, procurement and technical operations — often working between people, systems and decisions to turn detail into clear action.

That experience shaped a simple working instinct: understand the problem, separate fact from assumption, create the right structure and keep the work moving.
