How we handle personal information.

Updated for customer-journey remediation: 6 September 2026.

Who is responsible

Alan W Gallagher trading as House of Carol is the data controller. For privacy enquiries, email alan@houseofcarol.co.uk.

This website at present

The current website is not accepting customer enquiries or orders and does not contain an active customer contact form. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, special-category data or other unnecessary sensitive information through ordinary email.

If you contact House of Carol

Where an authorised contact route is opened, House of Carol will collect only the information reasonably needed to understand and handle the enquiry or relationship. The applicable purpose and lawful basis will depend on the actual interaction. Enquiry details will not be added to a marketing list without an appropriate lawful basis.

Service providers and transfers

Where an authorised service provider is used to process personal information, House of Carol will identify the relevant provider and safeguards in the live privacy information before that route is activated. The current website does not rely on an active Formspree customer-enquiry form.

How long we keep information

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably needed for the relevant purpose, legal or record-keeping obligation, or dispute. Information no longer required is deleted or anonymised where appropriate.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection law may give you rights to access, correct, erase or restrict your information, object to certain use, and receive certain information in a portable form. Contact House of Carol to exercise a right. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently use analytics, advertising trackers or non-essential cookies. If that changes, this notice and any necessary consent mechanism will be updated before use.
